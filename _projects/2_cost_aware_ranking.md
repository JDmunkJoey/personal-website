**Graduate Research Assistant, Department of Statistics & Data Science, WashU** · March 2026 – present
Advisor: Dr. Mengxin (Maxine) Yu

This project asks how to build a leaderboard that is valid for a specific target population when comparison data come from a different source population and labels are expensive to acquire.

- Formulated leaderboard estimation for a target population from mixed-size top-choice comparisons as a target-protocol weighted Plackett–Luce projection, keeping the estimand well defined under model misspecification and separating it from the source sampling design.
- Derived the efficient influence function and a cross-fitted one-step estimator under transportability, missing-at-random labeling, overlap, and local connectivity conditions.
- Reparameterized the semiparametric efficiency bound into a convex, cost-aware design problem yielding square-root allocation rules over comparison actions and expensive label acquisition.
